Dear [NAME],

Your 2021 study of Chamoli showed that a regional network could have detected that
cascade from up to 100 km away and warned within minutes, and it ended by noting that
real time use needs automated detection that does not yet exist. We have tried to build
that, for the 26 August 2026 Bhote Koshi cascade, using only the two open broadband
stations that recorded it. We would value your critical reading before we submit.

What we have, in short:

* A detector that runs on the live feed and decides about 2.6 minutes after onset. On
  the 2026 event that would have left about 35 minutes of warning at Bidur and 8 at
  Syabrubesi; Timure, 4 minutes downstream, cannot be warned by any detector.
* False alarms measured on noise corpora at the operating point: about 3 per station
  month at Kakani and 15 at the Everest station, counting the times the second station
  is missing. The rules that get it there, and what each one adds, are measured on one
  basis.
* A miss side estimate by injection: half of 2026 shaped events caught at about 2.5
  percent of its amplitude.
* A search for a second event in the region, including a long period scan of the 2015
  to 2016 temporary network, which found none.
* Tests on Bondo 2017 and Taan Fiord 2015. The low frequency tendency appears at both;
  the Nepal thresholds and the horizontal to vertical rule do not carry to Bondo.

Everything is open and reproducible from one command, including the negative results.

Three requests, in order of importance:

1. **A critical read of the draft.** We are most unsure about how we treat a positive
   class of one, and whether our claims are shaped correctly for it.
2. **Data.** If records from your seismometers along the Bhote Koshi after 2015 are
   archived and could be shared, they are the only way we know of to look for a second
   event in this valley.
3. **Collaboration.** If the work interests you, we would be glad to discuss coauthorship
   on the paper, or a joint test of the detector on events you know well.

The draft and the code are at [REPOSITORY LINK]. We are glad to share anything else.
